Mutilation Jigs

Mutilation covers for MBUSI assembly line.

SOLIDWORKSSurface ModelingTechnical InstructionsFDM 3D Printing

Problem

What I was solving

Doors were being scratched during assembly of upper ornamental trim. The team needed a quick jig for protection, but the engineer for that line had little to no CAD experience.

Process

How I got there

Extracted door surface from Siemens NX, built a cover around it in SOLIDWORKS, and 3D printed a prototype to validate fitment.

Solution

What I completed

Custom 3D printed covers for the door exterior until supplier could provide a long-term solution. Additionally, I created instructions for the line engineer to make similar covers for other parts in the future. Note that this version of the technical instructions was done with an arbitrary door model to protect proprietary information, but the original instructions were done with the actual door.

Results

Outcomes

  • →Reduced scratches by 60% across 3 weeks (15 scratches vs 6)
  • →Long-lasting impact by empowering the line engineer to make covers for other parts in the future
